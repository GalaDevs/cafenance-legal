Cafenance

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Cafenance is a sales, expense, cash and reporting app for coffee shops and other small food businesses. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who processes it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, profile photo, and the shop or team you belong to.
  • Business records you enter: sales, expenses, cash counts, inventory, staff, targets, notes, and report exports.
  • Location: the shop address and map coordinates you choose to add. The app asks for location permission only when you set a shop location.
  • Billing status: plan, trial dates, entitlement limits, and subscription state. Card and e-wallet details are collected and processed by our payment provider; Cafenance does not store them.
  • Usage and diagnostics needed to operate the service, such as sign-in events and error logs.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep your team's data separate.
  • To provide sales, expense, inventory, reporting, and optional AI assistant features.
  • To enforce plan limits, send billing and service notices, and provide support.
  • To detect abuse, maintain security, and meet legal obligations.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and server functions.
  • Xendit — payment processing for web subscriptions.
  • SendGrid — transactional email such as verification and billing notices.
  • Our AI provider — only the assistant request and the context needed to answer it.

These providers process data on our behalf under their service terms. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to shop records is limited by authentication and role-based rules, so a signed-in user only sees the shops and teams they belong to.

Retention

Shop data is retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward so you can export it. You can request deletion at any time.

Your choices

  • Export your data from the app.
  • Update your profile and shop details in Settings.
  • Manage billing emails from your account settings.
  • Request account deletion by emailing us; we delete or anonymize the data unless we must keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

Children

Cafenance is a business tool intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date and, when appropriate, notify you in the app or by email.

Contact

Privacy requests: privacy@cafenance.app.
