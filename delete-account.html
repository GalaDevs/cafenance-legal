Delete your Cafenance account

Applies to the Cafenance app (GalaDevs Tech Corp.)

You can request deletion of your Cafenance account and the shop data connected to it at any time. Follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@cafenance.app from the email address used for your Cafenance account.
  2. Use the subject line “Account deletion request”.
  3. Include your shop name so we can confirm the correct workspace.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, profile photo, role, and account credentials.
  • Your shop, branches, and team membership records.
  • Sales, expenses, cash counts, inventory, targets, notes, and reports created under your shop.
  • Uploaded files such as product and receipt images.
  • Assistant conversation history stored for your account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Billing and payment records are kept for the period required by tax and accounting rules (up to 10 years). They contain transaction amounts and references, not full card details.
  • Minimal security and abuse-prevention logs may be retained for up to 90 days.
  • Aggregated or anonymized statistics that cannot identify you may be kept.

Timing

We confirm receipt within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days, unless a legal retention obligation applies. If you are the shop owner, deleting your account also deletes the shop and its team data.
